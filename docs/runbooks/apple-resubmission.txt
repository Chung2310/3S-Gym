Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback. We have updated the app to allow independent gym
operators to register their own centers directly from the login screen.

Guideline 3.2

1. The updated service is available to independent gym operators and is not
   restricted to employees or partners of a single organization.
2. Any gym operator can register a center in the app without an invitation or
   manual approval. Each center has its own accounts and separate business data.
3. Gym operators can discover and download the app, register their center and
   administer their trainers and customer records. The app is intended for gym
   professionals; it is not a consumer gym-membership registration app.
4. From the login screen, select the center-registration option. The registrant
   receives an ADMIN account for their own center. That administrator creates PT
   accounts for trainers working at that center.
   Registration is also available from the login form using “Chưa có tài khoản?
   Đăng ký chủ gym”. Registration does not create a PT account.
