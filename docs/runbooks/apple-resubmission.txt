IMPLEMENTED PRODUCT FLOW
- Anyone may create an independent PT account from the mobile login screen.
- A PT can use their personal workspace immediately after signup.
- A gym admin invites an existing independent PT. Acceptance transfers all records
  in the personal workspace to the gym only after the PT confirms consent. The gym
  admin can then manage those records. Gym admins can also create PT accounts.
- Gym admin accounts are currently provisioned by a 3S SUPER_ADMIN. The app does
  not offer self-service gym-owner registration. Do not tell Apple that any gym
  can register its own admin account from the login screen.
- Mobile AI calls use company-paid fair-use limits. The mobile app does not show
  or debit the user's credit wallet. Web AI calls continue to use web credits.
  Production AI calls stop when the configured company monthly budget is reached.
- No email delivery or verification service exists in this project. Signup uses
  the existing IP-based rate limits; do not claim email verification.
